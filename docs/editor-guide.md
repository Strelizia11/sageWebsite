# Editing the UDD-SAGE website

Sign in at `/admin/login` with your provisioned administrator account.

## First-time content

1. Open **Organization & contact** and enter approved introduction, background, mission, vision, objectives and contacts. The supplied logo is used automatically; a library image can replace it.
2. Create an **Academic term** and designate it current.
3. Add people under **Members**, upload their photos and publish their profiles.
4. Assign the eight **Core officers** for that term.
5. Under **Committees**, assign one co-head and any members. The corresponding VP automatically appears as the head. A head cannot also be that committee's co-head.

Vacant positions stay visible as vacant. Changing the current term preserves historical assignments. A member with historical references cannot be deleted; use archived status instead.

## Publishing

Use **News & announcements**, **Examinations**, **Results** or **Activities & events**. Create the record, use a lowercase hyphenated slug, and save as draft. Preview the saved version in the editor. After checking the information, set visibility to published and save.

- **Draft:** only administrators can read it.
- **Published:** publicly readable and eligible for current listings.
- **Archived:** still publicly readable in archive views; omitted from current homepage sections.

Changing a published record updates the public page immediately after save. There is no revision history. The slug becomes fixed after first publication so existing links remain valid. Save conflicts prevent silent overwriting when another administrator has updated the same record; copy your changes, reload the latest version, then apply them again.

Markdown supports headings, lists, emphasis and links. Raw HTML is disabled. Do not paste scripts or embedded widgets.

## Files

Upload images or PDFs in **Media library**. Give images a meaningful description. After upload, select **Refresh available files** in the content editor; this updates file options without resetting your form or attachment changes. Results require an associated publicly visible examination and at least one ready PDF before publication. Gallery attachments appear in their configured order; use the up arrow to reorder.

Images: JPEG/PNG/WebP, maximum 5 MB. Documents: PDF, maximum 20 MB. Files remain private until attached to public content. Pending uploads can be deleted and retried. Detach a file from all content before deleting it.

Enter examination/event times in Manila time. Examinations themselves take place outside the website. Set cancellation explicitly if an examination is cancelled.

## Contact and links

Enter primary social page as a complete HTTPS URL. For additional social links and homepage quick links, use one `Label | https://example.com` entry per line. The contact page provides direct contact links rather than collecting messages.
